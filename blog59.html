```
Why Do Stock Traders Suggest to Always Set a Stop-Loss of 2% When Trading?

Understanding risk management is one of the most important steps toward becoming a disciplined trader.

```
Risk management is one of the most important aspects of trading.
Never risk more than 1% of your total account.

You must have heard it 1000000000000000 times till now.

I advise people to only risk 1% per trade to start. Once you learn and advance, you can bump it up to 2%.

You might be thinking:

“How am I ever going to become rich if I am just risking 1%? I will never double my money.”

If that’s your mindset, you might as well head to the casino and gamble your money away.

There really isn’t any difference between gambling and trading without risk management.
