What Is the Easiest Way to Make Good Money in the Stock Market in a Single Day?

Making good money in the Stock Market in a single day is possible. But the process is very complex.

If you take a Trade with the same mindset with which this question has been asked, then it is certainly not possible.

Now let's talk about how it can be possible.

For this, you don't need to Trade every day, but you need to stay connected with the Market consistently.

Notice that the Market does not give you a big opportunity every day. But every month or two, the Market does show one or two very big moves where a good opportunity can be created within just a few hours of movement.

How do you catch that move?

For this, you need to continuously monitor the Market.

THE HERON PRINCIPLE

Wait. Observe. Strike.

Have you ever seen a heron? If you haven't, just Google it. Because it is possible that it is known by some other name in your region.

If you observe a heron, you will notice that it can stand in one place near the water for hours.

It does not put its beak into the water to catch every fish.

It waits, and as soon as the right opportunity comes, it catches the fish in a single move.

Trading is also somewhat similar.

If you try to make money by taking 10–15 Trades every day, you may get caught up in small moves and end up losing your Capital through brokerage, wrong Entries, and Overtrading.

But if you continuously watch the Market, learn to identify good Setups, and wait for the right big move, then it is possible that just one big Trade in a month can generate a very good return for you.

THE BIG LESSON

The easiest way to make good money in the Stock Market is not to Trade every day, but to be prepared for the right day.

Money is not made in the Market every day.

But when the right opportunity comes, one day can give you a lot.

The only question is—

When that opportunity comes, will you be ready to identify it and catch it?

If you are, you may be able to make a good return in a single day.

Otherwise, it will be the same process again.

REMEMBER

“If you go to squeeze Profit out of the Market, the Market will squeeze you out instead.”
