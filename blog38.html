Can You Really Predict How Long a Stock Will Rise or Fall?

Is there really a method that can tell you exactly how many days a stock will continue rising or falling?

Let me ask you something.

Suppose you open a Weather App. It says that there is a possibility of rain for the next 7 days.

The next day, it actually rains.
It rains on the second day as well.
But on the third day, the sun comes out brightly.

Now does that mean the Weather Department was lying?

No, right?

It only gave a possibility based on the available information.

Just like the weather, the Market and its data can change every minute, and they do.

The Stock Market is exactly the same.

Analysis Gives Probability — Not Certainty

Whether it is...

📊 Technical Analysis
🏢 Fundamental Analysis
📈 Option Data
Remember
They tell you Probability.
Not Guarantee.

That is why sometimes even after having a completely correct Analysis, the Trade goes into Loss.

And sometimes, a Trade taken without any Analysis gives Profit.

Does that mean Analysis is useless?

Absolutely not.

It simply means that the future of the Market cannot be read.

But its probability can definitely be understood.

A Trader's Real Turning Point

From experience, I would say just one thing:

The day a Trader gives up the expectation that "I should already know how many days a Stock will run."

That is the day their Trading starts changing.

Because then their focus is not on knowing the future...

But on changing themselves with the changing Market.

And perhaps this is the biggest truth of the Share Market.

"The Market does not tell the future,
it only gives the next signal."

Stay real.

Living in reality is better than living in the world of illusions.
