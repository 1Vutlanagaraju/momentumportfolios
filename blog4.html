🛡️ Momentum Focus

HAL, Federal Bank and United Spirits are the cleaner momentum candidates. Prefer confirmation above the trigger instead of anticipating the breakout.

⚡ Higher Volatility

HSCL, BHEL and Adani Energy Solutions can move quickly in either direction. Position size should be reduced when the stop distance is wider.

📊 Confirmation First

For every setup, consider a sustained move or daily-close confirmation above entry resistance with supportive volume. Avoid chasing large gap-ups.

Weekly Trading Framework

  1. Enter only when the stated trigger is crossed with price confirmation; a trigger that never activates means no trade.
  2. Respect the stop loss. Risk only a small, predefined percentage of trading capital on each position.
  3. Consider partial profit at Target 1 and trail the remaining position toward Target 2.
  4. Exit early if the breakout fails, market structure deteriorates materially, or stock-specific news invalidates the setup.
